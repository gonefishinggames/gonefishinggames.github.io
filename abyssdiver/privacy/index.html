Idle Submarine: Abyss Diver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Idle Submarine: Abyss Diver is a single-player game published by Joshua Versace ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with your data. Contact: gfishingg.dev+support@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (dive position, weapons, currencies, purchases, daily counters) is stored only in the app's storage on your device.
  • Your settings (sound, effects, reminders) are stored on your device.
  • The game needs no account and does not upload your save. Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Delete my data, removes the save and settings.

Google services the game uses

Versions of the game that include these services use them as described here. They are not active in test builds that do not include them.

  • Google Play Billing processes purchases. Google receives the payment details; we receive only the product and order status needed to deliver the purchase. We never see card details.
  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (used to estimate general location), device identifiers (including the advertising ID and app set ID), ad interactions and diagnostic data, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one; the ad pass removes them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before any ad loads, and you can change your choice later.
  • Firebase Analytics records gameplay events: sessions, dives cleared, resurfaces, weapon draws, ad rewards claimed, purchases (product and price) and screens opened. Events carry no name, email or precise location.
  • Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports: stack trace, app state, device model, OS version and app version.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches settings such as ad limits and prices. It sends an installation identifier and basic device information (country, language, OS version), not personal data.

All of this data is encrypted in transit. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Ads: refuse or withdraw consent in the consent form where it is shown. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are requested.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete my data erases the local save and settings. Purchases stay with your Google Play account and can be restored from the Shop. To delete analytics or crash data linked to your installation, email gfishingg.dev+support@gmail.com.

Retention

Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, then deleted. Data on your device stays until you delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when anything changes and update the date at the top.
